      How it works[02]

      It lets go the moment your agent is done.

      Something for your hands while you wait, then a clear sign to go back.

      1. 01

        Send a prompt

        Claude Code starts working and clack wakes up, in full colour and sound.

      2. 02

        Play

        Crumple, pop, shave. No score and no streak, so nothing keeps you here.

      3. 03

        Go back

        When your agent is done or needs you, the picture fades to grey and the sound stops. That's your cue.

      Running several sessions? clack lets go when the first one finishes.

      Get clack[03]

      Three ways to have it.

      Keep it one click away in your Mac's menu bar, open it in your browser's side panel next to Claude or ChatGPT, or play it with nothing to install.

      Keep it in your menu bar

      • Follows Claude Code
      • macOS

      Its ring sits at the top right of the screen and opens clack from any app. Connected to Claude Code, it holds you while your agent works and lets go the moment it's done. Paste this into Terminal:

      curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kyuripark9/Keyboard/main/bridge/install.sh | bash

      It builds clack on your Mac in a few seconds and asks before connecting it to Claude Code. It needs Apple's Command Line Tools, and offers to install them if they're missing. Run the same line again to update. What it installs ↗

      Remove it
      curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kyuripark9/Keyboard/main/bridge/uninstall.sh | bash

      This quits the menu bar app, stops clack, takes its hooks back out of Claude Code and deletes its files.

      Open it in the side panel

      • Chrome · Edge · Brave
      • Any OS

      A browser extension that opens clack in the side panel, right next to claude.ai, ChatGPT or whatever you're waiting on.

      1. Download the extension and unzip it.
      2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
      3. Click Load unpacked and choose the clack-extension folder.
      4. Pin clack and click its ring: clack opens in the side panel.
      Download extension

      If clack is also on your Mac, the panel opens that one and follows your agent; otherwise the demo agent stands in.

      Play on the web

      • Nothing to install
      • Any browser

      All ten materials, right in the page, on a laptop or a phone. A demo agent stands in for yours, so you can feel clack let go.

      Open clack ↗

      To keep it as an app with its own window: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar; in Safari, choose File › Add to Dock.

      Privacy[04]

      It stays on your computer.

      Claude Code's hooks only tell clack the four things on this list, nothing else. clack listens on localhost alone, stores none of it and never sees your code. How quickly you went back is kept in your browser, and only ever shown as your last and typical return.

      What clack hears

      • You sent a prompt
      • The agent stopped
      • The agent needs you
      • A session closed

      What it never does

      • Listen beyond localhost
      • Store what it hears
      • See your code
