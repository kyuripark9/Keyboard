    How it works[02]

    It lets go the moment your agent is done.

    Something for your hands while you wait, then a clear sign to go back. Here a demo agent does the waiting, so you can try it now.

    1. 01

      Start a wait

      Click try an agent wait at the bottom of clack. A demo agent stands in for yours and works for twelve seconds.

    2. 02

      Play

      Crumple, pop, shave. No score and no streak, so nothing keeps you here.

    3. 03

      Go back

      When the demo agent is done, the picture fades to grey and the sound stops, even if you keep playing. That's your cue. Switch to another tab, and when you come back, clack shows how quickly you went.

    Try it now: open any material and click try an agent wait.

    Get clack[03]

    Pick where it lives.

    Play it right here with nothing to install, or keep it open in your browser's side panel beside whatever you're working in.

    Demo

    Both are demos: a pretend agent starts and finishes on its own, so you can feel clack let go. For the real thing, install clack on your Mac, where it follows your own coding agent and lets go of you the moment it's done.

    How to install ↗

    Web version

    Runs on
    Any browser, phones too
    Setup
    None

    All ten materials, full screen, on a laptop or a phone.

    Open clack →

    To keep it as an app with its own window: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar; in Safari, choose File › Add to Dock; on a phone, choose Add to Home Screen.

    Browser extension

    Runs on
    Chrome, Edge or Brave
    Setup
    Download, then load it by hand

    Opens clack in your browser's side panel, beside whatever you're working in. It isn't in the Chrome Web Store, so you add it by hand.

    Download extension
    1. Unzip the download. You'll get a clack-extension folder.
    2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
    3. Click Load unpacked and choose the clack-extension folder.
    4. Pin clack from the puzzle-piece menu and click its ring: clack opens in the side panel.
