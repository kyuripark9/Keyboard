A sound toy for agent waits

Something to press while your agent works.

clack puts ten satisfying materials under your cursor. Every step clicks, pops or squishes and leaves its own mark. Hand Claude Code a task and clack wakes up. The moment it's done, the colour drains away and clack lets go of you.

It lets go the moment your agent is done.

Waiting on an agent is a strange few minutes: too short to start anything, long enough to drift into a feed. clack gives your hands something to do, then hands you back.

  1. 01

    Hand off a task

    Send Claude Code a prompt and clack wakes up, in full colour and full sound. Its ring in the menu bar breathes while the agent works.

  2. 02

    Press something

    Crumple, pop, shave, rake. There is no score to beat and no streak to keep, so there is nothing to stay for.

  3. 03

    Let go

    When the agent finishes or needs your permission, the picture fades to grey over five seconds and the sound goes with it. The ring's dot fills in. That's your cue.

With several sessions at once, the first to finish lets go of you; once you've gone back, clack holds you again while the others work. In the browser a demo agent stands in for yours: press try an agent wait under the tally to feel the release.

Three ways to have it.

Play it here with nothing to install, keep it one click away in your Mac's menu bar, or open it in your browser's side panel next to Claude or ChatGPT.

Any browser · nothing to install

Play on the web

All ten materials, right in the page, on a laptop or a phone. A demo agent stands in for yours, so you can feel clack let go.

To keep it as an app with its own window: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar; in Safari, choose File › Add to Dock.

Open clack ↗

macOS · beside Claude Code

Keep it in your menu bar

Its ring sits at the top right of the screen and opens clack from any app. Connected to Claude Code, it holds you while your agent works and lets go the moment it's done. Paste this into Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kyuripark9/Keyboard/main/bridge/install.sh | bash

It builds clack on your Mac in a few seconds and asks before connecting it to Claude Code. It needs Apple's Command Line Tools, and offers to install them if they're missing. Run the same line again to update. What it installs ↗

Remove it
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kyuripark9/Keyboard/main/bridge/uninstall.sh | bash
This quits the menu bar app, stops clack, takes its hooks back out of Claude Code and deletes its files.

Chrome · Edge · Brave · any OS

Open it in the side panel

A browser extension that opens clack in the side panel, right next to claude.ai, ChatGPT or whatever you're waiting on.

  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the clack-extension folder.
  4. Pin clack and click its ring: clack opens in the side panel.
Download extension

If clack is also on your Mac, the panel opens that one and follows your agent; otherwise the demo agent stands in.

Ten materials, each with its own sound.

Each one is drawn after a print or a photo and synthesized live in the browser: no audio files, just soft attacks, rounded filters and small crisp details. Pick one to play it above.

    It stays on your computer.

    Claude Code's hooks only tell clack that you sent a prompt, that the agent stopped or needs you, and that a session closed. clack listens on localhost alone, stores none of it and never sees your code. How quickly you went back is kept in your browser, and only ever shown as your last and typical return.
