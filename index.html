01 — Why

Hands want something to do

We pop bubble wrap, squeeze slime and watch soap get shaved into cubes, not to finish anything but because the small, repeatable feeling settles us. ASMR videos turned these textures into a genre, and on Roblox, ASMR tower obbies such as 왁뿌볼 타워 and Keyboard ASMR Towers have players climb from one satisfying material to the next.

On a screen, though, we mostly watch. The textures are right there, but our hands never get to touch them.

What if the cursor were the fingertip?

02 — The idea

Every step is a press

The screen is laid out as a grid of invisible keys. Each cell the cursor crosses is pressed once: it plays a sound, and the picture under it reacts. Slow strokes tap, fast flicks sweep, and even an idle wiggle turns into a run of sound.

AJust move

No clicks, no goals, no score to lose. A quiet count of your clacks is the only number on screen.

BOne material, two senses

Each sound draws its own picture, and the picture answers where you touch it: bubbles flatten, snow takes a boot print, soap gives up a path of beads.

CSoft, never sharp

A quick stroke can fire twenty sounds a second, so anything with a hard edge (a ring, a ping, a thump) soon sounds like gunfire. The sounds are shaped from soft, filtered noise instead, so a stroke blends into one gentle wash.

03 — Materials

Ten materials, ten pictures

Each material starts from something people already find satisfying, and most pictures are drawn after a reference: a photo, a print or a texture. Pick one to play it.

04 — Process

It started as a keyboard

The first version was called 딸깍 (ddalkkak, the Korean word for a click): a full-screen keyboard that clicked under a passing cursor, with ten sound sets, from clicky switches to bubble wrap and marimba, and seven keycap themes. It took a lot of cutting to get from there to here.

  1. 1A keyboard that clicks

    Ten sound sets on a 60% keyboard. The name clack comes from here.

  2. 2Drop the keys

    Once the sounds turned into wax, slime and bubble wrap, a keycap was the wrong picture for them. Each sound got a picture of its own.

  3. 3Cut to two, rebuild to ten

    Most ideas didn't survive being played. The lineup shrank to Bubble Wrap and Crunchy Slime, then grew back to ten.

  4. 4Draw from life, tune for calm

    Pictures redrawn after photos and prints, and sounds softened until a fast stroke still feels calm.

Three iterations

06 Chewy Jellies

Tanghulu → Sugar Gummies → Chewy Jellies

Rubbing the sugar off a gummy wasn't the feeling. Squeezing gummies together, feeling them stick and peeling them apart was. Later the fruit colors and sugar gave way to one flat pink under a fine grain, after a pink and white reference.

03 Cyanotype

Ringing twinkle → bubble blips → soft blips → a breath of foam

Each try was softer, and each still sounded like shooting under a fast cursor. Filtered noise with slow attacks fixed it, and became the recipe for the calmer sounds that followed.

04 Wax Ball

Cell foam → pebbles → line print → towers → halftone → lime on lavender

Wax Ball went through eight looks and was cut once along the way. It came back as a black and white halftone of squares that the cursor presses into diamonds, then took the lime, lavender and fine grain of a second reference.

05 — Details

The small stuff

A cursor that belongs

The ring takes each picture's own color, so it reads as part of the material rather than laid over it.

No audio files

Every sound is synthesized live with the Web Audio API, with a little randomness, so no two steps sound exactly alike.

Your keys play too

Letters, digits, space and enter play the current material, and the arrow keys switch between materials.

Made for touch

On phones and tablets, drag a finger across the screen, and tap the arrows on either side of the logo to switch materials.

One moodboard

Cobalt, periwinkle, grey and warm paper, and ink. Six materials borrow their colors from their own reference images instead.

One file

The whole toy is a single HTML page: no build step, no install, nothing to load but fonts.
