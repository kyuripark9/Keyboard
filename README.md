# Clack

A stress-relief sound toy. Move your cursor across the screen and every step clicks, pops or squishes, and each sound draws its own picture to play with.

**Play it in your browser: https://kyuripark9.github.io/Keyboard/** (there a demo agent stands in for yours).

## Get it on your Mac

clack can sit beside your coding agent: it wakes when you hand Claude Code a task and lets go of you the moment it's done. Paste this into Terminal:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kyuripark9/Keyboard/main/bridge/install.sh | bash
```

It runs clack in the background, puts its ring in your menu bar, where one click opens clack from any app and the ring itself shows whether your agent is working or done, and offers to connect it to Claude Code. clack is also at http://localhost:4545, where Chrome or Safari can keep it as an app in your Dock. Everything stays on your Mac. See [bridge/README.md](bridge/README.md) for what it does, updating and removing it.

Sounds 04–09 follow the trend of Roblox ASMR tower obbies such as 왁뿌볼 타워 and Keyboard ASMR Towers, where every step lands on a different satisfying material.

- **Just move**: there is no start screen. The picture plays as your cursor moves, and the sound joins in after your first click, tap or key press, since browsers keep a page quiet until then. On touch screens, drag a finger. The page always opens on 01 Crunchy Slime.
- **Turn the page**: the arrows at the middle of each side of the screen go to the previous and next material, and the number by the logo (clack¹ to clack¹⁰) shows which one you're on. Nothing else sits on the picture: the logo, that number, the clack count and the arrows are drawn right on it in its own ink.
- **The story**: a case study for portfolio visitors, which nothing on the page opens for now: why it exists, how the cursor plays the screen, the ten materials with what each one looks and sounds like, the process from a clicking keyboard to ten materials (with the ideas that were cut), and the small details. Picking a material there plays it. The copy lives in the `story` markup and the `STORY` table in `index.html`.
- **Matching cursor**: a ring cursor that takes on each picture's own color.
- Your real keyboard plays too (letters, digits, space, enter), and the left and right arrow keys switch sounds. Your clack count is saved in the browser.

All sounds are synthesized live with the Web Audio API, so there are no audio files. They are tuned for ASMR: soft attacks, rounded filters, small crisp details and a little reverb. The palette comes from one moodboard: cobalt, periwinkle, grey and warm paper, and ink. 03 Bubble Wrap, 04 Wax Ball, 05 Thocky Keys, 06 Chewy Jellies, 07 Pudding, 08 Soap Cutting, 09 Snow and 10 Sugar Slime are the exceptions: each takes its colors from its own reference image or subject (fine white contour lines on sky blue, after a print of one; lime squares on lavender in a fine felt grain, from a grainy reference, over the rotated squares of a halftone; pale lime, blue, pink and orange on grey paper from a thermal print, on a photo of a perforated sheet; one flat pink on off-white under a fine grain, from a pink and white reference; green, red and cream; magenta halftone dots on cream, from a halftone print; fine olive and cream lines on periwinkle from a lenticular print; lime flecked with blue from a grainy gradient print).

## Run locally

It's a static page with no build step. Open `index.html`, or serve the folder:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000   # http://localhost:8000
```

## Deploy to GitHub Pages

In the repo, go to Settings → Pages, choose "Deploy from a branch", and pick this branch with the `/ (root)` folder.
